UPS & Inverter: your questions answered

How long will the backup last?

It depends on battery capacity and what you run. We size it for your target hours of backup.

Can I run an AC on a UPS?

Only with a properly sized inverter and battery bank. We advise realistic options.

How long do batteries last?

Typically 3 to 5 years with good care. We test battery health during every visit.
