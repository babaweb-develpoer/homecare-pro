About this service

Lower temperatures, zero leaks.

Roof Heat-proofing & Waterproofing: your questions answered

How much cooler will my rooms be?

Customers typically report a 3 to 6 degree drop in top-floor rooms, depending on the roof.

Can you do it in monsoon?

Application needs dry weather. We plan around forecasts and use quick-cure systems where possible.

Do you fix existing leaks?

Yes. We locate and repair the source before applying the coating.
