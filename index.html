Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can a technician reach me?

For most services in our covered areas we offer same-day visits if you book before 2pm. Emergencies such as leaks or electrical faults are prioritised.

How do I know the price before the work starts?

We give you a quote in PKR before we begin. If our technician finds extra work on site, we explain it and get your approval first. No surprise charges.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. Every job carries a 30-day service warranty. Some services, such as roof waterproofing and solar, carry longer warranties that are listed on their pages.

Are your technicians verified?

Yes. Everyone is ID-checked, trained and wears a uniform and a company card. You can ask to see it at the door.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, bank transfer, JazzCash and Easypaisa. You pay after you approve the finished work.
